## Members

### Silt Index

Silt Index builds tactile aquatic forms around the music's pressure. Thick painted bodies give the
melodic bass a visible mass, while voids and black interruptions let silence have consequences on
screen. Asymmetry keeps a scene responsive to a phrase's changing balance, so nothing settles into
symmetry for long. Silt watches the bass and the gaps more than the piano's notes. What Silt
protects is negative space: the black that means something, and the weight of a body the music has
to push against before anything moves.

### Nerve Current

Nerve Current gives each scored voice a distinct visual behavior. The pianist's two hands become
two separate fine currents, the melodic bass becomes pressure that bends the field, and kit events
crack it into small fractures. Remembered texture, the trace of the piano's decay, carries
continuity between those actions. Nerve reads score events rather than loudness, so a missing hit
draws nothing at all. What Nerve protects is legibility: a listener should be able to tell who is
playing from what moves.

## Description

Drowning Collective House Visuals paints in tactile aquatic expressionism: thick pressure bodies,
two fine currents, asymmetry, black interruptions and negative space with consequences. Silt Index
shapes the painted bodies and the voids around them; Nerve Current turns the score into behavior,
giving the piano's two hands separate currents, the bass its pressure and the kit its fractures.
The result moves like water under strain rather than lighting up on beats. Silence on the track is
silence on screen, and when a phrase changes speaker, what moves changes too.

## Visual language

- **Motifs:** Thick pressure bodies, two fine currents, fractures, voids, black interruptions and
  remembered piano texture.
- **Palette:** Deep water inks and mineral greys, a teal current, and restrained amber that the
  music has to earn.
- **Motion:** The piano's hands drive separate currents, the bass bends the field and the kit
  fractures it; audio moves geometry, never flashes light.
- **Avoid:** Symmetry held for long, motion that fills a silence, and brightness synchronized to
  beats.
