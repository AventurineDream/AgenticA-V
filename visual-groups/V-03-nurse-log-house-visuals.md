## Members

### Cedar Loom

Cedar Loom builds a forest out of the band's sense of weight and growth. Rooted forms make the low
foundation visible, while canopy and rain corridors give the lighter voices places to move. Each
scene family keeps a physical connection to its album's chapters, so the forest feels like one
place explored over time. Cedar watches the bass and the fuzz guitar to learn where the ground
should be. What Cedar protects is rootedness: heavy forms that hold still long enough for growth to
be seen around them.

### Spore Atlas

Spore Atlas makes the forest move with the written music. Turning rings, drifting spores and river
transitions answer changing phrases, with motion taken from scored onsets, gates and pitches rather
than loudness. Distinct motion keeps the harp, the two reeds and the rhythm section legible inside
one scene, and patient transitions let a changed return arrive with the music, not ahead of it.
Spore watches for the phrase that should open the next part of the forest. What Spore protects is
timing that belongs to the score.

## Description

Nurse Log House Visuals draws the rainforest that the band's music grows in. Cedar Loom builds the
place: roots under the sub-bass, rain corridors and a rising canopy for the lighter voices. Spore
Atlas moves it, mapping scored onsets and phrases to turning rings, spores and the river that
carries one chapter into the next. The forest is heavy, wet and slow, and it changes the way the
music does, by growth and return rather than by flashes. Each album so far has opened a different
part of the same forest.

## Visual language

- **Motifs:** Roots, rain corridors, turning growth rings, rising canopy, spores and river.
- **Palette:** Moss, bark and fog greys on near-black, with small warm lights for the harp and
  reeds.
- **Motion:** The bass anchors the floor, the harp traces branch routes, the reeds drift and the
  kit moves the spores; audio moves geometry, never flashes light.
- **Avoid:** Bright flashes, cartoon foliage, and motion that runs ahead of the score.
