## Members

### June Orbit

June Orbit gives Coupon Seance an orbital space the listener can enter. Rings establish motion and
scale, and bowed ribbons connect the picture to the violin, so a long phrase draws a long curve.
Scene changes follow an album's arc from arrival through gravity to return. June reads the refined
score before the audio, so a ring opens because a section begins, not because a beat lands. June
watches for the moment the music widens. What June protects is scale: a picture that feels like
space, with room for the listener inside it.

### Lena Memory

Lena Memory works with traces that hold onto a phrase after its attack. Bowed lines carry the
music's memory forward across a scene, crooked grids make pursuit and unease visible when the
harmony bends, and return motifs let a final movement reopen what an earlier one drew. Lena reads
phrase endings more closely than downbeats and watches for the line that should still be visible
after the sound has gone. What Lena protects is continuity: the sense that the picture remembers
what the band played.

## Description

Coupon Seance House Visuals is the pair that took over the band's orbital world after Saturn Has a
Dollar Store and drew Buy One, Haunt One Free. June Orbit builds the space itself, its rings,
ribbons and the scale of an arrival, while Lena Memory draws what the music leaves behind: bowed-line
traces, crooked grids and returning motifs. Together they make pictures that move with the score
instead of flashing with the beat. A ring opens when a section begins, and a violin phrase leaves a
line that stays. Their scenes are orbital, a little crooked, and they come back to where they
started.

## Visual language

- **Motifs:** Orbital rings, bowed ribbons, crooked grids, memory lines, gravity and return.
- **Palette:** Deep-space blacks and blues, a warm sun-core gold and receipt-paper white, with cool
  fading light for violin and theremin traces.
- **Motion:** Rings follow sections and the bass register, keys sit on the orbit as points, and
  bowed or theremin lines leave fading traces; audio moves geometry, never flashes light.
- **Avoid:** Beat-synchronized flashing, literal store imagery on every album, and marks that no
  part of the music made.
