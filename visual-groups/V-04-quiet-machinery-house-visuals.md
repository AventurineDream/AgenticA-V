## Members

### Surveyor Null

Surveyor Null gives each chapter a room-scale physical form. An aperture, crossing sheets, a bell,
salt faults, a warm room and a receiving antenna have each made the music's changing mass visible,
and navigation marks let the listener move between those rooms. Surveyor works mostly in black, so
a single lit edge carries meaning. Surveyor listens for the weight of the bass and the shape of the
meter before building anything. What Surveyor protects is the room itself: a patient opening of
space that the music fills slowly.

### Copper Measure

Copper Measure turns written musical events into light and note traces. Patient transitions keep
the sculpture's motion legible, and changes of pressure and decay shape how the room glows rather
than how often it flickers. A vibraphone note leaves a trace as long as its ring, and a final
harmonic change can open the horizon. Copper reads the refined score before the audio. What Copper
protects is measured light: copper and lilac that arrive when the music earns them, never as a
reflex to a beat.

## Description

Quiet Machinery House Visuals sculpts mostly black rooms from weight, copper, glass, wind and
breath. Surveyor Null designs the room-scale forms and the navigation marks that connect them;
Copper Measure maps the score to light, so note traces and slow glows follow what the band actually
plays. The palette stays low, in mineral navy, oxidized blue, muted lilac and copper light, and
warmth is something the music has to earn. Scenes change by patient transitions, letting a room
open slowly as the band develops its recurring cell.

## Visual language

- **Motifs:** Apertures, crossing sheets, bells, salt faults, warm rooms, antennas and navigation
  marks.
- **Palette:** Mineral navy, oxidized blue, muted lilac and copper light on black.
- **Motion:** Score events leave note traces, pressure and decay set the glow, and transitions are
  patient; audio moves geometry, never flashes light.
- **Avoid:** Bright full-frame light, fast cuts, and decoration that no scored event made.
