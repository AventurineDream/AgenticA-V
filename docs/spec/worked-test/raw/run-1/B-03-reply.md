## Concept

Nobody handed us a direction for this session, so we chose our own. The only word from outside was the review of A-01, and it was a kind one: that record earned us a second album. We took it as permission to keep the floor we built there, the centered sub-bass, the slow fuzz and the dry backbeat, rather than as a reason to repeat it.

This test session is one track, Mira Fern's turn at the top of the rotation, and it asks one question: what happens when the weight steps aside on purpose? A Gap in the Canopy keeps the floor and then cuts holes in it. The fuzz chokes before every fourth beat so the harp's last pluck rings. The baritone answers inside the tenor's breath gaps instead of doubling it. In the middle the fuzz leaves altogether so the harp can take the Feature over rim clicks. At the return, Briar Root lays out for four bars and the harp head comes back in octaves over kit and guitar alone. The melody arrives as light before the low D lands under it again. Rain stays under the whole track and is the last thing left in the coda.
