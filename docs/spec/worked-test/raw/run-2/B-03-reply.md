## Composition passes

### Pass 1 — Mira Fern

Wrote head A in D minor at 1/4=76: eight bars whose phrase entries fall on beat 4 or later, in the rest the core guitar rhythm leaves, so the harp starts where the fuzz stops. Set the form as Intro, A1, A2, B, a sixteen-bar harp Solo over the A changes, a return and a free coda, with a half-time groove under it.

### Pass 2 — Mira Fern

Added head B as two independent lines: the tenor phrases on the downbeats and rests at the ends of bars 2, 4 and 6, and the baritone moves in exactly those rests on its own rhythm, so the two reeds never double. Added the eight-bar Bari section over the B changes as Quinn Reed's open space, and swapped the A7sus4 in Solo bar 14 for Ebmaj7 so the solo's last turn leans darker before it resolves.

### Pass 3 — Mira Fern

A plain repeat of head A in the return had not earned its place, so I wrote head A3: the same first six bars, then a bar 7 that turns down onto E and a bar 8 that lands on a long D instead of the C-sharp turnaround. The chart's last two bars became Dm7 to match, and the coda was set at 25 seconds of free time so the landing has room to decay.

## Concept

Gap Dynamics starts from the other side: the moment the ground's cover breaks and light gets in.
