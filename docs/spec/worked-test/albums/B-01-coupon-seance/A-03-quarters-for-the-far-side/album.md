## Concept

The commission sent us to an all-night laundromat on the far side of the Moon, and we took it at its word. We have no drummer, so the machines keep time through us. Dale's 7/8 ostinato is a drum that never quite balances, and Sid's price-gun blips are the change machine taking a coin. The lost socks belong to Vera, whose violin carries what got left behind in the dryer and still remembers the trip.

"Start funny, end tender" became a shape inside one track before it becomes an album's arc. Rinse, Hiccup, Repeat opens on a joke: a one-eighth rest in the middle of the head, where the coin fails to drop. It ends with Vera playing that same hiccup at half speed, as a breath, over a bare theremin D and a trembling Ebmaj7 that never resolves.

We also heard our own Avoid bullet in the brief: this is not another store. The laundromat let us leave the discount-store orbit and keep what matters to us: the humor, the unease of a room lit all night with nobody in it, and the homesickness. This test pressing holds the opening track alone.

## Mastery

The Night Operator places Rinse, Hiccup, Repeat as the opener and only track of this test pressing. It starts after 1.5 seconds of silence, so the bass ostinato begins from nothing. There is no fade at the end: the violin's last release decays on its own, followed by 3 seconds of silence. The track sits at −16 LUFS integrated with true peak at −1.2 dBTP and a loudness range of about 12 LU. Its A2 level is the reference later tracks will be matched to, so the quiet coda keeps its full drop.

## Cover

- **A:** A single round machine door seen edge-on as a ringed planet, with a warm gold glow inside the glass against deep-space black.
- **B:** One mismatched sock drifting in low orbit around a dim grey Moon, trailing a pale curved thread like a bowed line.
- **C:** Rows of round machine doors receding down a dark blue corridor, the farthest one lit warm gold, its reflection bent into a crooked grid.
- **Chosen:** B — the sock carries the tenderness the track ends on, and its thread draws Vera's coda spiral without showing a store.
