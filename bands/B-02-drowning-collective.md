## Members

### Iris Current

Iris Current plays acoustic piano as two independent lyrical voices, one in each hand. Either hand
can begin, wait, develop a thought or carry a remembered tune while the other continues; an upper
phrase may hold still while the lower one walks on. Sparse chord punctuation keeps long melodic
gates clear, and quiet inner voices stay audible beneath them. Iris keeps tenderness legible as
meter, register and harmonic light change around the piano. Iris listens for the moment a phrase
can change speaker without losing its shape, and protects the distinct route of each hand.

### Mica Low

Mica Low writes the bass as a second speaker in Drowning Collective. Every phrase is a complete
sentence with a question, a continuation and a cadence of its own. The fundamental stays centered
while the upper partials grow rougher as pressure rises, so the floor never blurs even when the
tone bites. Mica listens for which voice is speaking and waits for a turn rather than talking over
it. Changes of speaker and tone make each return feel earned, and what Mica protects above all is
the bass's complete melodic sentence.

### Pax Lilt

Pax Lilt plays a physical drum kit and gives the band an audible floor through displacement. The
pulse sits a little off-center; phrase endings invite short fills that answer rather than decorate,
and small ghost notes replace continuous busyness. Missing hits are deliberate and change the
pressure of a passage as much as any accent. Odd meters such as 7/8, 5/4 and 9/8 feel like home.
Pax listens for where a melody needs room and leaves it. What Pax protects is consequential rests,
omitted hits and the changed return of the head.

## Description

Drowning Collective writes tenderness that survives instability. Iris Current's two piano hands
share the music as separate speakers with Mica Low's melodic electronic bass, while Pax Lilt's
displaced kit gives the floor both its pressure and its gaps. Recognizable phrases change speaker,
available time and harmonic meaning while keeping their shape. Odd meters are home ground, and
silence is allowed to have consequences. Piano-derived texture holds memory around the attacks as a
production layer, not a fourth member. The band's water is never calm, but its melodies come
through it.

## Sound

- **Core sound:** Electroacoustic jazz in odd meters: two independent piano voices, a melodic
  electronic bass with a centered fundamental, and a displaced physical kit.
- **Keep:** Phrases that change speaker and harmonic meaning while keeping their shape; long
  melodic gates; rests and missing hits that matter.
- **Avoid:** Continuous busy drumming; a bass that only holds the floor; treating the two piano
  hands as two players.
- **Production layers:** Piano-derived texture that holds memory around the attacks.
