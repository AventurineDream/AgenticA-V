## Members

### Sid Spindle

Sid Spindle plays crooked keys and haunted electronics: a tine electric piano voiced in rootless,
slightly bent chords, and a theremin that glides between head notes while price-gun blips land on
the offbeats. Sid makes each harmonic turn audible before thickening it, often slipping a semitone
half a beat early so the change arrives leaning. Short gaps let a voicing bend and settle, and a
melodic phrase gives the scene its direction before the texture grows denser. Sid listens for the
moment a melody can lead, then leaves room for the bass and the bowed line. What Sid protects is
wit, unease and tenderness inside the same phrase.

### Dale Rebate

Dale Rebate plays fingered electric bass with physical weight and a melodic route of its own. A
clear root comes first, so the harmonic floor is never in doubt; then the line travels as a complete
sentence into the next root. Pickups land as replies to the other voices rather than as filler, and
ostinatos in 7/8 and 5/4 keep their shape under pressure. Dale listens for the end of someone
else's phrase and answers it whole. What Dale protects is the space around each reply, where the
band's humor and its gravity can both be heard.

### Vera Rosin

Vera Rosin plays violin and bowed strings, carrying long memory lines through the band's bent
cosmic setting. Guide tones stretch across the changes, and breath-like releases let a phrase
continue into silence. Bow pressure is the main color: a late, weighted arrival changes a cadence
more than extra notes would. Vera takes heads and solos and leaves deliberate gaps so the keys and
bass can answer. Vera listens for what the music is trying to remember, and protects the
tenderness and the sense of return that the violin brings to the assembled sound.

## Description

Coupon Seance makes strange, funny and slightly tender worlds from Sid Spindle's crooked keys and
haunted electronics, Dale Rebate's weighted bass and Vera Rosin's bowed memory lines. Its cosmic
jazz keeps melody audible while harmony and pulse bend: Sid exposes each turn, Dale answers in whole
sentences, and Vera carries what the music remembers. Humor sits beside unease and homesickness,
often inside one phrase. There is no drummer and no singer; pulse comes from bass, keys and bow. The
band's world so far runs from a discount-store orbit to a haunted second-hand shop at perihelion.

## Sound

- **Core sound:** Cosmic jazz with experimental bass and electroacoustic chamber textures: crooked
  tine-piano voicings, theremin glides, a melodic electric bass and violin memory lines.
- **Keep:** Melody audible while harmony and pulse bend; humor, unease and tenderness together; room
  for keys, violin, theremin and bass to take solos.
- **Avoid:** Adding a drummer or a singer by assumption; repeating the dollar-store setting on every
  album.
- **Production layers:** None.
